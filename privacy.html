Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or log in to use Tollgate.
  • Your Screen Time data stays on your phone. We never see which apps you block or how you use them.
  • Your camera feed never leaves your phone. Reps are counted on your device, and video is never recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • We collect only what's needed to run the app, process subscriptions, and understand whether people find the app useful.

Who we are

Tollgate ("Tollgate", "we", "us") is an iPhone app made by an independent developer in the United States. This policy covers the Tollgate iOS app and this website. If you have questions, email hello@YOURDOMAIN.com.

Information the app keeps on your device only

These stay on your iPhone. We cannot see them, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

  • Screen Time selections and usage. Tollgate uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls) to block the apps you choose. Apple gives Tollgate only private, encrypted references to those apps, not their names. Your selections, schedules and usage information are used only to provide Tollgate's features. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and never used for advertising.
  • Camera. When you choose to earn time, Tollgate uses your camera to detect body position and count your reps. This happens entirely on your device. Video and images are never recorded, saved or transmitted.
  • Your settings and progress. Your gates (schedules), earned minutes, rep history and preferences are stored on your device.
  • Notifications. Tollgate's notifications are created on your device. We don't use push notification servers.

Information we collect

Anonymous app analytics

To learn which features help people and where the app is confusing, Tollgate sends a small number of anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. Examples: "onboarding completed", "gate created", "reps completed", "paywall viewed".

  • These signals do not include your name, email, Apple ID, location, which apps you block, or how you use other apps.
  • Identifiers are anonymized before they leave your device, so signals can't be linked back to you.
  • They are never used for advertising and never shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. To manage subscriptions (for example, to check whether Premium is active or restore a purchase), we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic technical details such as app version, device type and country.

Waitlist and email

If you join the waitlist on this website, we collect your email address. It is stored with our email provider, Kit, and used only to send you Tollgate news: a confirmation email, a welcome email, occasional build updates and the launch announcement. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and we never sell or share your email.

Website analytics

This website may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies and without tracking you across other sites.

Support emails

If you email us, we use your email and message only to reply and help you.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't share Screen Time or usage data with anyone.

How long we keep information

  • On-device data is kept until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Anonymous analytics and purchase records are kept only as long as needed to run and improve Tollgate, or as required by law.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off Tollgate's Screen Time or camera access at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. Email hello@YOURDOMAIN.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Tollgate is designed for adults managing their own phone use. It is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll also let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@YOURDOMAIN.com.
